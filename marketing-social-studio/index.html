01 / Outcomes

Build from the audience outward.

The studio keeps research, message strategy, content production, and copy review separate enough to inspect.

  1. 01Know who this is for

    Ground the audience in current evidence, context, behavior, and a real decision.

  2. 02Choose the message

    Connect the audience’s job to a defensible value proposition and an honest call to action.

  3. 03Ship the right shape

    Adapt the story to the channel, then check specificity, voice, sources, and reader trust.

02 / Practice

Research, frame, make, check.

Use the specialist that matches the current job, then bring its evidence into the next pass.

A

Current audience research

Separate current signals from assumptions and identify what still needs checking.

B

Communication story

Find the tension, reader value, proof, and next action that make the message coherent.

C

Channel craft

Build platform-appropriate posts, campaigns, briefs, and content sequences without copy-paste sameness.

D

Humanizer gate

Remove generic praise and unsupported certainty while preserving the writer’s real cadence.

03 / Example

Make the research visible in the brief.

A bounded prompt makes the requested change and its evidence inspectable.

Starting prompt

Build a launch brief for this workshop.\n\nIdentify the audience decision and evidence we have. Propose one message spine and three channel-specific posts. Use Humanizer last. Do not invent urgency, outcomes, testimonials, or audience data.

Useful return

An evidence ledger, one clear audience-and-action brief, three genuinely adapted posts, and a short list of claims that still need support.

04 / Fit

Use it for the work it can actually support.

Good fit

Launch briefs, campaign framing, social posts, community updates, teaching promotions, content systems, and public portfolio communication.

Not a substitute for

Current research, community knowledge, legal review, platform policy, consent, or a measurement plan tied to the real goal.
