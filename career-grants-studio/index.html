Public Claude plugin / Career evidence

Build the case. Keep the evidence honest.

Career & Grants Studio turns source material into resumes, letters, portfolio cases, and grant-process plans without inventing metrics, ownership, outcomes, or certainty the record cannot support.

SourceRoleResult

01 / Outcomes

Make each claim traceable.

The studio starts with the evidence packet and the audience’s decision, then builds the document.

  1. 01Inventory the proof

    Separate verified facts, reasonable framing, missing evidence, and claims that should not be used.

  2. 02Match the decision

    Connect relevant evidence to the role, reviewer, funder, or portfolio reader in front of you.

  3. 03Finish the artifact

    Produce a focused resume, letter, case study, or grant-process plan with remaining questions visible.

02 / Practice

Four artifacts, one evidence rule.

Use the specialist that matches the current job, then bring its evidence into the next pass.

A

Resume

Prioritize role-relevant evidence, accurate scope, concrete contribution, and readable hierarchy.

B

Cover letter

Make a short, specific case for fit instead of paraphrasing the resume or praising the company.

C

Portfolio case

Show the situation, your decisions, artifacts, collaboration, evidence, limits, and what changed.

D

Grant process

Turn requirements, roles, evidence, dependencies, and review gates into a finishable submission path.

03 / Example

Ask what can be defended first.

A bounded prompt makes the requested change and its evidence inspectable.

Starting prompt

Use the source packet and job description below.\n\nBuild a claim ledger before drafting. Mark every metric, ownership claim, and outcome as supported, needs clarification, or exclude. Then produce a one-page resume. Do not fill gaps with plausible detail.

Useful return

A source-linked claim ledger, a role-specific draft, a short exclusion list, and the questions whose answers would materially strengthen the application.

04 / Fit

Use it for the work it can actually support.

Good fit

Resume tailoring, cover letters, portfolio case studies, grant-process planning, evidence audits, and reviewer-ready application packets.

Not a substitute for

Your factual review, confidential-data judgment, funder requirements, legal advice, references, or a final human read of the submission.
