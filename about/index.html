Why the Compass Suite exists

The Compass Suite gives AI assistants narrower jobs and stronger boundaries. Instead of asking a model to generally critique everything, each Compass focuses the work: reasoning, prompt design, UX review, or another bounded workflow.

What makes the tools different

The public Compasses are local-first, evidence-aware, and designed around explicit validation. They are not magic judges. They are structured helpers that make the review process easier to inspect, repeat, and improve.

Official release boundary

Official Compass releases are published by Jonathan Kyle Hobson through the linked public pages and GitHub repos. Modified forks should keep attribution, but they should not present themselves as official Compass Suite releases.
